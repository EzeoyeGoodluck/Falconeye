When speed matters

Air cargo

The fastest way to get your goods between Nigeria and the UK.

  • Ideal for boxes, suitcases and urgent items
  • Shorter transit times than sea freight
  • Competitive rates, priced on weight
Best value for big loads

Sea cargo

The most cost-effective option for large, heavy or bulky shipments.

  • Fully loaded cars and vans
  • Barrels, equipment and large quantities of boxes
  • Lower cost for heavy and high-volume goods
