Shipping options

Two ways to ship

When speed matters

Air cargo

The fastest way to get your goods between Nigeria and the UK.

  • Ideal for boxes, suitcases and urgent items
  • Shorter transit times than sea freight
  • Competitive rates, priced on weight
Best value for big loads

Sea cargo

The most cost-effective option for large, heavy or bulky shipments.

  • Fully loaded cars and vans
  • Barrels, equipment and large quantities of boxes
  • Lower cost for heavy and high-volume goods

Before you ship

Packing tips

Good packing keeps your goods safe and helps your shipment move quickly.

  • Use strong boxes and tapeDouble-wall boxes for heavy items; seal every seam.
  • Label everything clearlySender and receiver names and phone numbers on each item.
  • Keep a list of contentsIt helps with customs and with tracking your items.
  • Ask before you packSome items can't be shipped. Check with us if you're unsure.
