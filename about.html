About us

Connecting Nigeria and the UK, one shipment at a time

Who we are

Logistics you can rely on

Falconeye Logistics and Couriers provides air and sea cargo services between Nigeria and the UK. We ship everything from boxes and suitcases to equipment, barrels and fully loaded cars and vans.

Our goal is simple: fast, reliable delivery at the best value rates, with affordable pickup options so shipping is easy from the very first step. When you call us, you speak to people who know your shipment.
