Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Call +44 7954 510070 or email falconeyelogistics@gmail.com.

Shipping

Do you ship in both directions?

Yes. We ship from the UK to Nigeria and from Nigeria to the UK, by air and by sea.

What can I send?

Boxes and suitcases, equipment, barrels, and fully loaded cars and vans. If you're not sure about an item, ask us before you pack it.

Should I choose air or sea?

Air cargo is faster and suits boxes, suitcases and urgent items. Sea cargo is better value for heavy or bulky goods such as cars, vans, barrels and equipment. We'll recommend the best option when you ask for a quote.

How long does shipping take?

It depends on whether you ship by air or sea and on the current schedule. We'll give you an up-to-date estimate with your quote.

How is the price worked out?

Prices depend on the size and weight of your goods, air or sea, and whether you need a pickup. Contact us for a free quote.

Can you collect from my address?

Yes. We offer affordable pickup options from your home or business.

Are there items you can't ship?

Yes. Some goods, such as flammable or hazardous items, can't be shipped. Check with us before packing.

Drivers

Do I need my own vehicle?

Yes. We work with self-employed drivers who have their own car, small van, large van or refrigerated van.

How are drivers paid?

Per delivery. We agree rates and payment terms with you before you start.

Ready to ship?

Tell us what you're sending and we'll come back with a price.
