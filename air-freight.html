Air freight

Air freight when speed matters

Fast, reliable air cargo from the UK to Nigeria, from Nigeria to the UK and from the UK to Ghana, at competitive rates.

Get a free quote
Why air

The quickest way to get it there

Air freight is the fastest way to move goods between the UK and West Africa. It suits smaller shipments and anything that can't wait, and it's priced on weight so you only pay for what you send.

  • Shorter transit timesYour goods arrive sooner than they would by sea.
  • Priced on weightClear, competitive rates with no surprises.
  • Pickup from your doorAffordable collection from your home or business.
  • Updates along the wayOur team keeps you informed until your goods are delivered.
What we fly

Boxes and suitcases

Personal belongings, gifts, clothing and household items.

Urgent items

Documents, parts and goods that need to arrive quickly.

Business shipments

  1. Get a quote
