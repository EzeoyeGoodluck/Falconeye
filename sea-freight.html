Sea freight

Sea freight for big and heavy loads

The most cost-effective way to ship large, heavy or bulky goods from the UK to Nigeria, from Nigeria to the UK and from the UK to Ghana.

Get a free quote
Why sea

Best value for big loads

When you're sending a lot, or sending something heavy, sea freight keeps the cost down. It's the natural choice for barrels, equipment, vehicles and large quantities of boxes.

  • Lower cost for heavy goodsPay far less per kilo than air for big shipments.
  • Room for large loadsBarrels, equipment, furniture and boxes in bulk.
  • Pickup from your doorAffordable collection from your home or business.
  • Updates along the wayOur team keeps you informed from pickup to delivery.

Barrels

How it works

Sea freight in four steps

  1. Get a quote

    Tell us what you're sending, roughly how much, and where it's going.

  2. Book your pickup

    We collect from your address, or you can drop off.

  3. We ship it

  4. Delivered
